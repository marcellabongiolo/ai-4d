01 / THE CONCEPT

What could AI look like when time becomes part of the model?

In this project, “4D” is used as a conceptual model: three spatial dimensions plus time. The goal is to explore how intelligent systems can reason about evolving environments rather than treating every observation as an isolated snapshot.

AI 4D is a portfolio prototype—not a claim that “4D AI” is a single established scientific field. It is a visual and technical playground for ideas around temporal data, spatial computing, simulation and machine learning.
